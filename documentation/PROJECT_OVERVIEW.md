# Cyber Raksha AI — Project Overview

## 1. Vision & Purpose
**Cyber Raksha AI** is an intelligent cybersecurity threat analysis platform architected to detect, analyze, explain, and mitigate modern digital threats using artificial intelligence and machine learning.

The name "Raksha" stems from the Sanskrit word for *protection* and *defense*, embodying the platform's core mission: empowering individuals, organizations, and national digital infrastructure against sophisticated cyber attacks.

---

## 2. Platform Core Pillars (Planned Future Capabilities)
1. **AI-Powered Phishing Detection**: Advanced NLP transformer pipeline detecting credential theft, spear-phishing, urgency heuristics, and homoglyphs.
2. **Malicious URL & Website Detection**: Lexical, structural, domain-age, and certificate reputation analysis detecting phishing landing pages and malware delivery domains.
3. **Voice Cloning & Audio Impersonation Detection**: Deep acoustic spectrogram and neural vocoder artifact detection defending against vishing and CEO telephonic fraud.
4. **Suspicious Communication Analysis**: Multimodal contextual analysis flagging social engineering, extortion, and fraudulent wire instructions.
5. **Multi-Layer Fraud-Risk Assessment**: Holistic Bayesian threat scoring across multiple signals to yield high-fidelity risk indices.
6. **Explainable AI (XAI) Threat Analysis**: Human-understandable threat reasoning and actionable mitigation playbooks.
7. **Threat Monitoring & Prevention Recommendations**: Continuous posture tracking, incident response guidance, and preventive controls.
8. **Web & Android Cross-Platform Clients**: Desktop operations center alongside mobile defense application.

## 3. Stage 1 Scope: Architecture & Project Foundation
Stage 1 lays the complete architectural bedrock:
- ✅ Zero-dependency core stack (open-source only).
- ✅ FastAPI REST backend with active health pinging.
- ✅ SQLAlchemy ORM with SQLite database (PostgreSQL-ready).
- ✅ React 19 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS frontend.
- ✅ Futuristic cybersecurity aesthetic (Deep Navy, Midnight Blue, Cyan accents).
- ✅ Three.js 3D visualization framework prepared for telemetry rendering.
- ✅ Real-time live status synchronization without fake stats or mocked metrics.
- ✅ Modular AI Engine directory ready for training pipelines in Stage 2.
- ✅ Pytest suite validating contracts, database persistence, and system endpoints.
